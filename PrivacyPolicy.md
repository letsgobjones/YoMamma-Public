# Privacy Policy for Yo! Mamma

**Last Updated:** November 23, 2026

**Introduction**

Thank you for using Yo! Mamma! This policy explains how Brandon Jones ("we," "us") handles your information when you use our mobile app (the "Application").

**In short:** Yo! Mamma has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your sessions, tasks, plans and settings stay on your device, and we do not receive them. The camera is used only to follow your eyes during focus sessions, and nothing it sees is recorded, saved or sent.

**Information Stored on Your Device**

The Application keeps the following on your device so the app can work:

*   Your chosen study buddy, the name you give (optional) and the title you wear.
*   Your focus sessions, tasks, study plans and check-ins.
*   Your gold, the items you have bought, how your room is decorated, and your achievements and streaks.
*   Your settings, such as the timer length, voice, tone, tracking switches and notification times.

A small summary (your streak, today's minutes, what's next and your buddy's room) is shared with the Application's own widgets on your device so they can show it.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is kept for as long as the Application remains installed, and deleting the Application deletes it. You can also remove your sessions and tasks at any time in Settings > Delete history.

**Information We Collect**

We do not collect any personal information through the Application, and we do not run a server that receives your data.

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us, so we can reply.

We do not collect location data, contacts or photos.

**Camera and Face Data**

On iPhones with Face ID, the Application uses the front (TrueDepth) camera during focus sessions, and in the Crew Lab screen in Settings, to see where your eyes are looking. This lets your study buddy's eyes follow yours and lets the Application warn you when you look at your phone.

*   The face and eye information is processed on your device, in memory, only while it is being used.
*   No photos or video are taken, and no face data is recorded, stored, sent to us or shared with anyone.
*   It is not used to identify you, for advertising, or for any other purpose.

The Application asks for camera permission first. You can turn eye tracking off in the Application's Settings, or turn off camera access for the Application in iOS Settings.

**Motion Sensors**

During focus sessions, the Application reads your device's motion sensors to notice when the phone is picked up. This happens on your device and nothing is stored or sent. You can turn it off in Settings.

**Microphone and Speech Recognition**

When you tap the microphone button to say what you want to work on, the Application uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to turn your words into text. The microphone is used only while the button is on, and audio is not stored by the Application. When your device supports it, recognition happens on your device. Otherwise your audio is sent to Apple to be recognised, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We never receive it.

**Apple Intelligence**

Live comments during sessions and the Break it down feature use Apple Intelligence on your device. Your task names and session details are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

**Notifications**

Check-in notifications are optional. If you allow them, the Application schedules them on your device at the times you choose. They are created and delivered by your device, not by a server. You can change the times in Settings, or turn off notifications for the Application in iOS Settings.

**Sharing**

If you tap a Share button, the Application creates a picture (for example, of a badge, your streak or your week) on your device. It goes only where you choose to send it through the iOS share sheet.

**In-App Gold**

Gold is earned by focusing and spent in the in-app shop. It cannot be bought with real money, and the Application does not handle any payment information.

**Information From Apple**

Apple may give us aggregated information about the Application, such as downloads and crash reports, from users who agreed to share it with developers in their device settings. It does not identify you.

**How We Use Information**

*   To respond to support requests.
*   To understand and fix problems you report.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

**Third-Party Access**

We do not share your information with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

**Opt-Out Rights**

Uninstall the Application to remove everything it has stored on your device.

**Data Retention**

We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you. Contact us at yomamma.app@gmail.com to delete your support-related data.

Data stored on your device is retained as long as the Application remains installed.

**Children's Privacy**

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. You must be the minimum age of consent, as defined by your nation's laws, to submit personal information through the Application.

**Security**

Your information stays on your device, so we encourage you to keep your device safe and protected by a passcode. We take reasonable steps to protect any data we handle, but no method is 100% secure.

**Changes**

We may update this policy, and we will change the date above when we do. If we ever add features that collect information, such as accounts, syncing, purchases, advertising or analytics, we will update this policy to describe them. Continued use means you accept the changes.

**Your Consent**

By using the Application, you consent to this policy.

**Contact Us**

Contact us at yomamma.app@gmail.com with any questions.
