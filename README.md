# Support for Yo! Mamma: Study With a Buddy Who's Got Your Back

Welcome to the support page for Yo! Mamma, the study app where a cartoon study buddy sits with you while you focus, cheers you on, and lets you know when you've picked up your phone.

Yo! Mamma is free, works on iPhone and iPad (iOS 27.2 or later), and has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## What You Can Do

*   **Pick a study buddy:** Sunny the Cheerleader, Rio the Chill Friend, Coach Jim the Drill Sergeant or Mira the Balanced Mom. Reeves, the Steady Lawman, is a secret you can unlock.
*   **Choose how they talk to you:** Gentle, Tough love ("Yo mamma" style teasing) or Mix it up.
*   **Focus sessions:** 10 to 60 minutes at a table in your own room. Your buddy reacts to how the session goes, and warns you if you stare at your phone or pick it up.
*   **Plan your day:** a Today list, steps for big tasks (Break it down), and Plan tomorrow.
*   **Study plans:** turn a goal like "14 chapters by December 12" into a small step for each study day.
*   **Progress:** streaks, a weekly chart, a year heatmap and every session you've done.
*   **Earn gold and decorate:** focusing earns gold. Spend it in the shop on rooms, decor and pets for your buddy's room.
*   **Achievements and titles:** badges for things like your first hour, a 7-day streak or a perfect week.
*   **Share cards:** pictures of a badge, your streak, a finished plan or your week.
*   **Widgets and reminders:** Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and check-ins from your buddy at times you choose.

## Getting Started

*   **Find your study buddy:** swipe through the crew on first launch. Each one introduces themselves.
*   **Set up a first plan:** say or type what you want to work on, pick a finish date and your study days, and choose how your buddy should talk to you.
*   **Tap Start:** set your phone down and focus. When the timer ends, tell your buddy how it went.
*   **Come back daily:** keep your streak going and pick up the deal of the day in the shop.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

*   **How does my buddy know I'm looking at my phone?**
    *   On iPhones with Face ID, the front camera follows your eyes during a focus session. Look at the screen for more than about a second and a half and your buddy warns you. Nothing is recorded or saved. Turn it off in **Settings > Eyes on you** (**Follow my eyes** and **Warn me when I look at my phone**). If you're using your phone to study, choose **Using it to study** to pause the warnings for 10 minutes.
*   **How does it know I picked up my phone?**
    *   It uses the phone's motion sensors. Set your phone down when you start a session. Turn it off with **Yell when I pick up my phone** in **Settings**.
*   **How do I change my study buddy, or how they talk to me?**
    *   Open **Settings**. Your study buddy is at the top, and the **Voice**, **Tone** and **Live comments** settings are in the card about how your buddy talks.
*   **How do I earn gold?**
    *   You earn 2 gold for every focused minute, 10 extra for your first finished session of the day, and 25 for each achievement. Pauses and breaks don't count. Gold is only for the in-app shop and can't be bought with real money.
*   **How do I unlock Reeves?**
    *   Save up 1,500 gold and buy him in the shop.
*   **How do I decorate my room?**
    *   Tap **Decorate** on Home. Drag decor to move it and pinch to resize it. Pets you buy have their own bed or bowl to sleep in.
*   **What are the live comments and Break it down?**
    *   Short remarks from your buddy during a session, and a way to split a big task into steps. Both use Apple Intelligence on your device, so they need a device that supports it with Apple Intelligence turned on.
*   **How do I add a widget?**
    *   Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap **Edit** (or **Customize** on the Lock Screen), then add Yo! Mamma. Tap any widget to start focusing.
*   **How do I change or turn off the check-in notifications?**
    *   Change the times or switch off **Daily check-ins** in **Settings**, or turn off notifications for Yo! Mamma in iOS Settings.
*   **Is my progress saved to the cloud?**
    *   Not yet. Everything is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it doesn't move to a new phone automatically.
*   **How do I clear my history?**
    *   Open **Settings** and choose **Delete history**. This removes your sessions and tasks and can't be undone.

## Troubleshooting

*   **Check for updates:** make sure the app is up to date in the App Store.
*   **Restart the app:** if something looks stuck, close the app and open it again. If it crashes, please send a bug report.
*   **No sound?** Check the **Voice** switch in **Settings** and your volume. The once-a-day opening chant stays quiet when the silent switch is on.
*   **No phone warnings?** Eye tracking needs an iPhone with Face ID and camera permission (iOS Settings > Apps > Yo! Mamma > Camera). Pick-up detection needs a real iPhone resting on a table.
*   **The microphone button doesn't work?** Allow the microphone and speech recognition for Yo! Mamma in iOS Settings. You can always type instead.
*   **No live comments?** Make sure Apple Intelligence is turned on in iOS Settings and your device supports it.
